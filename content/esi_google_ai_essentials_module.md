Module Overview
This module introduces practical generative AI skills for everyday work. Learners explore how AI tools can improve productivity, how to write clear prompts, and how to evaluate risks such as bias, inaccurate output, privacy concerns, and security issues. These skills support ESI employees across roles by helping them select appropriate AI use cases, maintain human oversight, and integrate responsible AI practices into workflows and the AI-powered software development cycle.

Learning Objectives
- Identify workplace tasks that can benefit from generative AI tools.
- Evaluate whether AI is appropriate for a specific task or workflow.
- Apply effective prompting techniques to produce clear and useful outputs.
- Recognize potential bias, privacy, security, and accuracy risks in AI use.
- Integrate human review and responsible AI practices into daily work.
