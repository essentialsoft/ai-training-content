## Module Overview

This training module provides a practical foundation for building and evaluating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. You will learn how to design production-ready RAG architectures and implement robust AI evaluation methodologies. By mastering a five-step development framework and understanding how to measure AI quality, you can ensure your AI applications are accurate and reliable. These concepts directly integrate into ESI's AI-powered software development cycle, equipping engineers and product teams to confidently deploy, monitor, and optimize enterprise-grade generative AI solutions.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify common pitfalls in AI development and evaluation methodologies.
- Build a baseline Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system using a five-step framework.
- Create a golden dataset to systematically evaluate model outputs and retrieval accuracy.
- Evaluate AI applications effectively to ensure reliability in production environments.
