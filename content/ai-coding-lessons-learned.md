# Lessons Learned: 6 Months of AI Coding

**Difficulty** : Intermediate

**Topics** : AI in Software Development, Workflow

## Module Overview

This module explores how AI coding tools like Cursor, Bolt, and Lovable shift the traditional bottlenecks in software development. By analyzing the experiences of a 20-person development team over six months, you will learn how the engineering landscape is evolving. These insights integrate directly with ESI's AI-powered development cycle, empowering technical leads and developers to adapt their workflows, hire the right skills, and streamline team performance for future project success.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the shifting bottlenecks in modern software development caused by AI tools.
- Evaluate the impact of AI coding assistants on team productivity and workflow.
- Optimize hiring strategies to align with the new skills required for AI-assisted engineering.
- Apply practical insights from a real-world development team to ESI's AI-powered software cycle.
